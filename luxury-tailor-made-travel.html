Luxury Trains in India

India especially recognized in the world for its luxury train experiences. Customized India Trips can take you back in the era of the kings and royalty. Maharaja Express is the most luxurious train in India and runs between the October and April. It gives experiences such as meeting the member of the royal families, elephant polo match in Jaipur and visiting the UNESCO Heritage Site Khajuraho Group of Temples. Place on wheels and Royal Rajasthan On Wheels travel on similar routes that starts in New India and covers the Jaipur, Sawai Madhopur, and Chittorgarh. Don't miss these trains if you are coming in India!
