Local sightseeing in Nainital:-

Nainital is a very famous destination specially for honeymoon for couples, family vacation, and for holiday for office trips, Easy Cab India helps you solve travel problems through our customized plans. The following spots are famous in Nainital to explore when you choose our car rental service :-

Naina Devi Temple

Naina devi temple is the main and religious destination in the Nainital which is situated near Naini lake and thus attracts thousands of devotees throughout the year to seek the blessings of goddess Naina Devi.

Naini Lake:-

Naini lake is the heart of the Nainital and the most visited tourist destination in the Nainital. Here you can enjoy photography, boating and enjoy the great green scenery of the mountains.

Snow view point

It offers you the panoramic view of the snow covered Himalaya and also offers you to take blessings of Nanda Devi.

Himalaya Darshan:-

It offers you the expansive view of the Himalaya mountain range with the help of a telescope.

Lovers point:-

known for its romantic backdrop and green scenery.

Eco cave garden:-

It is the best spot for the families and kids in which you can enjoy six interconnected rocky caves with hanging gardens.

Suicide point:-

It offers you plunging view of the valley below which is very uneven and dramatic.
