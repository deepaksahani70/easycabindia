Local sightseeing in Kullu

As Kullu is famous for its beautiful places like temples,valleys, waterfalls,snow covered mountains and its markets, it becomes important to book car hire in Kullu to explore all these beautiful places with our taxi service without any hassle, our car rental includes experienced drivers will cover all these sightseeing around Kullu in a customized plan. They will guide you to the best tour sites with the best hotel stay and dining spots. Some famous and attractive sites are:-

Bijli Mahahdev temple:-

To reach this temple, car hire service is the best option. It is located at a high altitude and so offers an amazing and mesmerising panoramic view of the valley.

Raghunath temple:-

This temple is dedicated to lord Ram and holds a great religious significance which attracts devotees from various places.

Great Himalayan National Park:-

This park is rich in biodiversity and houses the rarest of rarest wild flora and fauna. Nature lovers and wildlife supporters enjoy visiting this great himalayan national park.

River rafting in Beas river

After rafting, your car hire service will take you back safely for the travellers in the Beas river. Our taxi service will conveniently drop you to the rafting point.

Manikaran:-

It is situated 40 km from Kullu in the Parvati valley,it is famous for its hot sulfur spring and landscape.

Tirthan valley:-

It is known for its trout fishing,apple orchard and is a very peaceful place to enjoy the natural beauty of the Kullu.
