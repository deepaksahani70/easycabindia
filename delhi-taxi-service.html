Car Rental and Taxi Service in Delhi

Delhi is the busy and historic National Capital Territory of India. Ancient architecture and the modern built environment can be found side by side. It is the political, cultural, and economic heart of the country. It is a city where the essence of a dynamic "mini-India" can be found, with each of its many traditions and religions woven together and with every kind of food found throughout the country.

India Gate

A memorial arch that is 42 meters tall in memory of the members of the British Indian Army who died in the World War (1914-1918).

Akshardham Temple

A Hindu temple complex that is huge and contains incredible stone work of India’s ancient history and carvings. It contains musical fountains that perform in the evening.

Lotus Temple

The Lotus Temple is a flower-shaped location of worship as well as a location for visitors to relax and meditate.

Chandni Chowk

This market's location right next to the Red Fort offers Indian Culture to the visitor through its street foods and artisan crafts.

Red Fort (Lal Kila)

Indo-Islamic architectural style is evident in the design of this fort commissioned by Shah Jahan in the 17th century and in 2007 became a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Humayun’s Tomb

The design of the Taj Mahal was inspired by this vast tomb in a garden which contains the remains of the Mughal Emperor Humayun.

Qutb Minar

This tower is a 12th century construction by Qutb ud-Din Aibak, the tallest brick Minaret in the world and is a member of the UNESCO Heritage sites.

For tourists, Delhi is a prime destination as it is the top place to learn about the rich cultural traditions of India, and offers world-famous monuments, a diverse art scene, and exceptional street food. Its massive land size is one of the reasons they can't see all of Delhi. Tourists can suffer from a lack of the right guidance, or suffer from the notorious Delhi traffic.
That is the reason, taxi services are vital components of the city, enabling convenient point-to-point transit and local exploration.
