Local sightseeing in Ludhiana

Iskcon Temple:-

peaceful spiritual spots and community serving. People come here to seek blessings and peace.

Gurudwara Manji Sahib{Alamgir}:-

a historical and spiritual site located about 10 km from the city.

If you are history and culture lover, you can visit following spots:-

Maharaja Ranjit Singh War Museum:

it mainly shows military history of Punjab and honors Indian defense forces.

Phillaur Fort:

Designed by Dewan Mokham Chand, best architectural historical fort for history lovers

Lodhi Fort (Purana Qila):

Again if you want to explore the nature and leisure in the Ludhiana you must visit:-

Nehru rose garden:-

It has hundreds of rose varieties and water fountains and thus attracts visitors for its lush green spaces and peaceful environment.

Rakh Bagh Park:-

Popular green area of the city which has jogging and cycling space and mini toy train for the kids.

Hardy’s world Amusement Park:-

It offers thrilling water slides and family friendly rides.

Tiger Safari and Zoo:-

The most visited place for wildlife enthusiasts which offers you to see caged animals and chance to spot tigers on safari rides.

Chaura Bazaar:-

Best place to explore the Punjabi culture and enjoy the best Punjabi Foods.

We offer you to explore all these local sightseeing with comfort and ease as our experienced and verified drivers will guide you the best place and timing of the visit so that you can enjoy the beautiful places without any hassle.
