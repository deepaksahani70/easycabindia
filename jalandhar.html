Local sightseeing in Jalandhar

Jalandhar has many tourist spots, historical locations,shopping areas and cultural destinations which are worth visiting. Easy Cab India helps you to explore all these local sightseeing with comfortable and affordable rides. Popular sightseeing in Jalandhar includes:-

Devi Talab Mandir

A 200-year-old temple complex and one of the 51 Shakti Peethas. It has a large holy tank and a beautifully illuminated shrine at night.

Jang-e-Azadi Memorial

Located in Kartarpur (just outside Jalandhar), this memorial captures Punjab’s massive contributions to India's freedom struggle.

Gurudwara Talhan Sahib Ji

A renowned, marble-white historic shrine known for its peaceful aura and the annual fair held to honor Shaheed Baba Nihal Singh.

Rangla Punjab Haveli

The perfect place to experience traditional Punjabi culture, folk dances, and for enjoying the perfect Punjabi foods.

Pushpa Gujral Science City

Located on the Jalandhar-Kapurthala road, it has an interactive educational hub featuring a space theater, a dinosaur park, and laser shows.

Wonderland Theme Park

A family amusement park which has amazing thrilling dry rides, a massive water park, and entertainment areas suitable for all ages.

Nikku Park

an amusement park that is great for taking a relaxed evening walk and treating young children to basic rides.
