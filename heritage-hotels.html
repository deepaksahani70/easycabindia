Heritage Hotels in India – Royal Palace Stays & Luxury Heritage Vacations

India is the destination of heritage and culture. One can experience this amazing heritage in the several monuments and forts noticeably, which are scattered throughout India. Heritage Tour in India let you experience the services of Heritage Hotels. These are the places where you will get the treatment of kings and Maharajas. Easy Cab India help you to stay in one of many Heritage Hotels in India where you will get the royal lifestyle and let you explore culture and heritage as well as reservoirs art. Some of them are UNESCO World Heritage Sites due to their architecture, excellence, and historical significance.

Heritage Hotels in Rajasthan

A Jaipur-pink city gives Luxurious Retreat in terms of the Assistant haveli which consists of the traditional several courtyards, arches, alcoves, pavilions and ornate artwork which is enhanced with the gold. Jagat Niwas Hotel in Udaipur with Lake Pichola views was used for the royal banquettes. It is popular for the mirror work, colorful murals, antique types of furniture and newly installed roofs.

On the other hand, Jodhpur and Bikaner consist of heritage mentions that were ruled by maharajas. Narendrbhawan in Bikaner is an unconventional and captivating that gives the experience of the maharaja's life. It’s a heaven for foodies and lets you explore the sundowners in the desert hinterland. Ratan Vilas in Jodhpur is very warm and welcoming. It includes the swimming pools and garden restaurant where you can date with your partner under the stars.

Heritage Hotels in India

Haveli Dharampura in India is the heart of the Chandni Chowk in India and was derelict before rescued by the heritage enthusiastic. Its restoration was recognized by the UNESCO. These heritage hotels were once residence of Kings, and influential people of the city. They have inherited amazing art pieces, paintings, architecture, and majestic design that looks from outside as well. These are the heritage hotels in India where culture is preserved with the comfort and convenience which make you feel at-pleasure. Each heritage hotel in India feature old heritage and grand feels
