Mussoorie is a hill station located in Garhwal region of Uttrakhand. It has beautiful green trees that look like a green blanket covered on hills with mesmerizing waterfalls and towering mountain peaks. It is the most liked and visited place for vacation,honeymoon, and weekend planning. You can explore a wonderful view of the Himalayan snow ranges to the north-east and the Doon Valley. Musoorie is also called the “Queen of Hills” and because of its green hills and scenic beauty, tourists are more attracted here to explore this natural beautiful hill station.

Service offered by Easy Cab India in Musoorie

Local sightseeing taxi service in Musoorie

As Musoorie is filled with natural beautiful scenery and hills with amazing waterfalls and many more things that are enough to attract everyone, here we will help you to explore all these sightseeing in a single day or step by step on different days whatever you plan according to your stay in Musoorie. Popular attraction sites in Musoorie includes:-

Kempty falls

It is the most famous,beautiful and mesmerising waterfall in North India. You can enjoy this beautiful waterfall with your family and friends in a comfortable taxi with us.

Gun Hill

It offers stunning views of Himalaya and Doon valley. It is the second highest point in Musoorie and accessible through a ropeway ride from Mall road.

Camel’s back road

The name is due to the resemblance of a camel's back. It is the most peaceful place for relaxing, morning walk and enjoying the Himalaya view.

Company Garden

Well known for boating,colourful beautiful garden ideal for enjoying and relaxing

Lal Tibba

It is the highest point in Mussoorie. With Easy Cab India you will be able to explore unmatched, panoramic views of the snow-capped Himalayan peaks like Bandarpoonch and Nanda Devi.

Mall road

It is the main attractive point of any hill station. You can enjoy shopping,dining and evening walks. It connects the library chowk and Kulri bazaar.

George Everest House

Short trekking from the parking area, you can enjoy the ultimate view of Doon valley from here.
