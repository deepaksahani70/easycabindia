Delhi Sightseeing Cabs: What Nobody Tells You Before You Book One

Let Me Start With Something Honest

I live in Delhi. Not as a tourist. As someone who has seen people make the same mistake every single weekend. They book a hotel in some random place like Karol Bagh. Then they think "I will just take an auto to India Gate." Then they reach India Gate. Then they realise – India Gate to Lotus Temple is 12 km. In Delhi traffic, that's 45 minutes. In an auto, without AC. In 42 degree heat. By 2 PM, they are tired. Their phone battery is dead. They are arguing with a second auto wala about 30 rupees. And they still have to see Qutub Minar. This is not a holiday. This is a struggle. That is why car rental for Delhi sightseeing exists. Not to make you feel rich. To make you feel sane.

What Most Websites Won't Tell You About Delhi Darshan Cabs

I run a small cab service called EasyCabIndia. Not a big brand. Not a fancy app. Just some cars, some good drivers, and one simple rule – you tell us where to go, we take you there. No fight. No hidden charges.

But before you book any cab – even with us – there are three things you should know.

First: A cheap cab is often expensive in other ways.

Someone offers you Delhi sightseeing cabs for ₹1800 for full day. Sounds good. Then the driver says "sir parking extra" – ₹300. Then "sir toll extra" – ₹150. Then "sir night charge" – ₹400. Suddenly your ₹1800 cab is ₹2650.

We don't do that. Our price is fixed. What you see is what you pay.

Second: The driver matters more than the car.

A brand new Innova is useless if the driver doesn't know which gate of Red Fort is open for dropping passengers.

Our drivers have been driving in Delhi for minimum 5 years. Some have done 10 years. They know the shortcuts through Mahrauli. They know where to wait near Chandni Chowk without getting a challan.

Third: You cannot cover 10 places in one day.

I don't care what any itinerary on the internet says.

Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Raj Ghat, India Gate, Lotus Temple, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, Akshardham – all in one day? Not possible.

You will rush. You will not enjoy. You will take bad photos. And you will be angry at the end.

Better to pick 4 or 5 places. See them properly. Eat a proper lunch. Take a break. That is real sightseeing. And that is exactly why reliable car hire for delhi sightseeing makes all the difference.

So What Does a Real Delhi Sightseeing Cab Look Like?

Let me give you a normal day – from our actual customer last month. Family of four from Chennai. They booked our 12 hour Delhi darshan cab package.

  • 8:00 AM – Our driver Sanjay reaches their hotel in Paharganj 10 minutes early. He calls them. "Madam, main neeche hoon, car mein AC on hai, aaram se aao."
  • 8:30 AM – They leave for Qutub Minar. Sanjay takes the route from Aurobindo Marg. Less traffic.
  • 9:15 AM – Reach Qutub Minar. Sanjay says "main ticket counter ke paas wait karunga, jitna time chahiye utna lo."
  • 10:45 AM – They come back. Next stop Lotus Temple.
  • 11:15 AM – Reach Lotus Temple. Sanjay drops them at the exact walking path. Not 500 meters away like some drivers do.
  • 12:15 PM – They finish. Now they are hungry. Sanjay suggests a clean AC restaurant near Nehru Place. Not a expensive one. A normal one where families eat.
  • 1:15 PM – Lunch done. Next stop Humayun's Tomb.
  • 2:00 PM – Reach Humayun's Tomb. Sanjay explains "two hours enough hai, phir India Gate ja sakte hain."
  • 4:00 PM – They finish Humayun's Tomb. Then India Gate for photos. Then they ask "can we see Red Fort from outside only?" Sanjay says yes.
  • 6:00 PM – They want to go back to hotel because children are tired. Sanjay drops them.

Total places seen: 5. Total frustration: zero.

They paid ₹3200 for the full day. Divided by 4 people = ₹800 per person. For 10 hours of AC car, a polite driver, no bargaining, no sweating. That is what a proper taxi service for delhi sightseeing looks like.

Tell me honestly – is that expensive?

Why Our Delhi Darshan Cabs Are Different

I am not going to give you marketing language. No "best in class" or "unparalleled service". Let me just list what you actually get when you book car rental for delhi sightseeing with EasyCabIndia.

  • Real local drivers. Not some person who came from another state last month and is learning Delhi roads on your time. Our drivers live in Delhi. They have families here. They know the city like the back of their hand.
  • AC cars that actually work. I know this sounds basic. But you will be surprised how many "AC cabs" have weak cooling. We check our cars every morning. If AC is not cold, that car does not go out.
  • Fixed price printed on paper. When our driver meets you, he carries a small slip. On that slip, your total price is written. No extra charges. No "sir parking management fee." Nothing.
  • You make your own itinerary. Some cab companies force you to follow their "Delhi darshan package." We don't.
  • 24/7 support number. If your cab is late, if you forget something in the car, if you want to extend the booking – you call a Delhi number. A real person picks up.

Common Questions (People Ask Us Every Week)

Q: Can we start very early like 6 AM?

A: Yes. We have drivers available from 5 AM. No extra charge for early morning. Just tell us when booking.

Q: Do drivers speak English?

A: Basic English. They can understand "stop here", "wait 30 minutes", "take us to Qutub Minar". For full English, request when booking.

Q: Can we keep luggage in the car while sightseeing?

A: Yes. If you are checking out from hotel on the same day, you can keep all luggage in the car. We don't charge extra for that.

Q: What if we finish early?

A: Then you can go back to hotel early. No refund for unused hours. That is standard.

Q: Is there any hidden charge?

A: No. I have said this three times already. But people still ask. So let me say it again – No. The price you see is the price you pay.
