Rajasthan - ( Land Of The Kings )

Rajasthan is known as the “Land of the kings.” It is rich in food, heritage sites, and beautiful locals. It is not only famous for beauty and heritage but also popular for some exciting activities. It has the highest number of forts and palaces in India. It is one of the most colorful and vibrant states. Golden sand deserts, traditional handicrafts, and awe-inspiring places make Rajasthan an ideal spot for a vacation. Luxury car travel makes your Rajasthan trip memorable. Don’t forget to say “Khamma Ghani” when you reach there.

Heritage Places and Fort

Rajasthan has some of the best places and forts in India. These forts have something that you never have seen before. Jaisalmer Fort is one of the best places to visit in India. It has an awe-inspiring architecture. The mesmerizing view of going down Sun can make you feel amazing! The City Palace of Jaipur is the tourist attraction. The majestic residence of the Royals can give you several reasons to visit the palace. Just experience the Royal life there! Jal Mahal and Jantar Mantar are just masterpieces of the Rajasthan. Jal Mahal is located near to Man Sagar Lake and offers breathtaking views. Jantar Mantar is one of the World Heritage Sites in India that you must experience the charm by self. Amer Fort is classic and its exclusive design can awe-inspire! Hawa Mahal and Sheesh Mahal are some special attractions. The interior of the sheesh Mahal is made largely of the mirror and with the part of the light palace. Hawa Mahal has architecture exceptionality where you can experience watching the street of the Jaipur. Other popular forts are Jugrah Fort, Nahargarh Fort, and Chittorgarh Fort. Trip to forts gives the best cultural experience ever!

Luxurious heritage hotels in Rajasthan

Umaid Bhavan palace has a very unique and heritage architecture. It offers a heritage walk. It is run by the Taj group of hotels. Oberai Udaivilas is located near to Lake Pichola and offers a spectacular view across the lake. It’s one of the world-famous hotels. Samode Haveli is located in the heart city of India. It has beautiful gardens, terraces, and a yard. When it comes to living royal life, Shiv Nivas Palace is the best for it. It offers world-class comfort and top-notch services. Amet Haveli is the finest heritage hotel that offers personalized services and warm hospitality. The best Heritage Hotel in the “Heritage Grand” category is The Fateh parkas’ palace. It offers Traditional Marwari Hospitality with luxurious facilities and excellent services. The Narain Niwas Hotel and Garbaland Palace are also famous heritage hotels in Rajasthan. Heritage hotels make your trip memorable!

Heritage train in Rajasthan

The palace of the wheel is the luxury train that designs to reflect the royal grandeur of the erstwhile kings of the Rajputana kingdom. It has modern facilities and amenities which give comfort and entertainment. The Maharaja Express voted as the ‘The World’s Leading Luxury train’ in the year 2012. It offers world-class accommodation, facilities, and services. Each room is filled with modern amenities and facilities. Another luxurious train is The Royal Rajasthan on Wheels. It has excellent interiors, great mils, fabulous services, and excellent wines. It takes you to heritage, tourist and wildlife places in Rajasthan. When it comes to Luxury train you can’t miss Deccan Odessy. It undertakes a weeklong journey and goes through Udaipur, Jaipur, Agra, and India- A Golden Triangle Tour.

Art and Culture of Rajasthan

Rajasthan is among those places which have a high essence of creativity and artistic colors. Observe the soul-stirring art and culture and see art design using stones, leather, wood, silver, textile, and handicrafts. Witness the folk culture and hear folk songs that tell the stories of the Kings and Queens. Experience the colorful culture with traditional clothes, footwear, and jewelry. Rajasthan is also famous for its fairs and festivals. Some of the vibrant festivals are Gangaur Festival, Camel Festival, and Desert Festival. During the trip also enjoy the Pushkar fair, Khatu Shyam Ji Fair and Karni Mata Fair. The fair and festival add the colors to the vibrant land.

Food of Rajasthan

Rajasthani Food is famous for its several vegetarian dishes. All the cuisine is rich in ghee and spices. Dal Bati Churma and Gatte ki Sabji are popular among the tourist. It also has meat carries such as Laal Maas and Safed Maas. You will get the authentic Rajasthani Meal at Chokhi Dhani. Jheel’s Ginger Coffee Bar and Bakery are famous for the smoking frappe, sandwiches and delicious coffee with spectacular view o Lake Pichola. In the end, don’t miss the Special Lassi that makes you say, “Yummee!”

Activities to do in Rajasthan

Camel Safari in the desert is the best thing to do in the Bikaner. It’s one of the best experiences that you must take. Camping in the Bikaner is also famous among international tourists. Don’t come back without Shopping in Rajasthan! Shopping at Jodhpur’s colorful street market must be on your trip. You will get Bandhej, one of the tie and die saris. Other famous places for shopping are Johri Bajar in Jaipur, Bada Bajar in Udaipur and Clock Town Market in Jodhpur. Watching the Pink City through the Air Balloon can be the memorable moments of your trip. It’s just a magical adventure which starts from the Jal Mahal, Amer Fort, and Shiv Vilas. In the end, See the tigers and other animals at Ranthambore National Park and Sariska Tiger Reserve. Take the trip now!
