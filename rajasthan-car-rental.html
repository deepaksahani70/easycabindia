Here's What We Offer in Rajasthan

1. Jaipur Sightseeing – The Pink City

Most people start their Rajasthan trip from Jaipur. Hawa Mahal, Amer Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar – lots to see, lots to do.

Our Jaipur local package gives you 8 hours or 80 km, whichever comes first. We'll pick you up from your hotel, airport, or railway station – your call. The driver actually knows the shortcuts, which saves you at least an hour. You get a clean sedan or SUV, whatever you prefer. And waiting time is included at each monument, so no rushing.

Our drivers know one thing for sure – Amer Fort is a nightmare after 10 AM. So go early. Like before 9 AM early. Less crowd. Better photos. Pleasant weather. And don't worry – we'll get you there.

2. Udaipur Honeymoon Package – The City of Lakes

Udaipur? Very romantic. But imagine this – you're sitting in the back, trying to have a moment, and your driver is singing along to his phone. Yeah. Not great. That's why we do things differently.

Alright, here's what's waiting for you. Fresh flowers in the car – yes, we actually do that. Your own playlist, we set up Bluetooth before you arrive. Our drivers are trained to give you space – no staring, no random questions. You can stop wherever you want. Want to spend an extra hour at Fateh Sagar Lake? Go ahead. The driver waits. And for sunset at Ambrai Ghat, our drivers know exactly where to park.

  • Fresh flowers in the car
  • Bluetooth music setup
  • Privacy-focused drivers
  • Flexible sightseeing stops
  • Parking assistance at Ambrai Ghat

"Yaar, the flower thing made my wife cry. Happy tears. Thank you for that."

3. Jodhpur Business Travel – The Blue City

Jodhpur isn't just tourism anymore. Textile companies, handicrafts, defense – lots of business happens here.

  • Premium sedans like Honda City or Hyundai Verna
  • Silent drives
  • 15-minute early arrival
  • GST invoices
  • Corporate monthly billing

4. Jaisalmer Outstation – The Golden City

Jaisalmer is far. From Jaipur? 550 km. From Jodhpur? 300 km.

  • Well-maintained SUVs
  • Safety breaks every 4 hours
  • Actual toll & parking charges
  • Same driver for round trip

At the time of booking, we provide you with complete transparency regarding all details. The base fare covers the driver's salary, fuel, and vehicle maintenance.
