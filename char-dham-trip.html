Char Dham - The Char Dham is a Set of Four Pilgrimage Sites in India.

It has great significance in Hindi Religion. The pilgrimage site located at Badrinath, Puri, Rameshwaram, and Dwarka. It consists of the three Vaishnava locations and one Shiva. These places are mentioned in the Hindu texts such as Vedas and Puranas. It would take you through the most picturesque landscape which includes the forests, valleys, waterfalls and river confluences. The Chardham Yatra is more rejuvenating and spiritually uplifting. It is also called the Chardham of Himalayas band consist of four scared sites Badrinath, Kedarnath, Gangotri, and Yamnotri. Take this trip to get the blessings of God!

Chhota Char Dham Places

Badrinath is the five Badri temples consisting of the Yog Dhyan Badri, Adi Bhadri, Bhavishya Badri and Vridh Badri, connect with the Badrinath Temple. It is located at an altitude of 3415mts above. You may hear about its stunning beauty, forest, pleasant weather, snow-clad mountains and Holy River with surrounding green valley. The temple is full of devotees during the Mahashivratri festival. Kedarnath is located at the bank of the Mandakini River at an altitude of 3584 meters. It is not only the part of Char Dham but one of the 12 jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva in India. It offers breathtaking views and scenic beauty that attracts the tourist from all over the world. Gangotri is the original source of the river Ganga. It is located at an altitude of 3100 meters above sea level. It opens the auspicious day of Akshay Titya and closed on Bhai Duj. This time is May to November and it stays closed the rest of the six months. Yamunotri is located at an altitude of the 3185 Mt and has religious importance. It is famous for the springs and glaciers. It is the source of the Yamuna River. Devotees all around the world used to offer pooja at the temple itself.
