Leh Ladakh Adventure Tour, Nature Trip & Monastery Tour in Ladakh

If you are looking for an amazing trip then you will explore the important sightseeing of Ladakh. It is full of fresh air that makes you feel like the new birth! Whenever you look, you will see the natural grace with the full of snowpack the mountain landscape, pristine lakes, and unique culture. Trek along with sky-high 4 big mountain ranges! The best time to visit Ladakh is from March to October. Ladakh is especially known for Leh Palace, Hemis Monastery, Cham Dance in Hemis Festival and Buddhist Heritage. Easy Cab India offers exquisite adventure Tour Packages for Ladakh. Ladakh is one of the dream places for world travelers. Make your trip now!

Buddhist Monasteries in Ladakh

Hemis Monastery is an 11th century Tibetan Monastery in Ladakh. It is popular for its Buddhist heritage and collection of Buddhist relics as well as items like weapons and paintings. Hemis Festival is enjoyed by the native people and tourists as well. Indigenous people dress up in the lovely attires and gather for Cham Dance in the country yard of the Hemis Monastery. It is celebrated on the 11th of July to 12th July every year. This key Monastery is a beautiful red and white color establishment on the top of hills. It is one of the biggest monasteries in Ladakh. Don’t miss to visit the Diskit Monastery which is one of the important places in Buddhism. It is one of the oldest monasteries in the Nubra Valley. It offers a beautiful view of surrounding hills, rivers and mountains. Some other monasteries are Shey Monastery and Spituk Monastery.

Famous Lakes of Ladakh

You may have never seen something like Pangong Tso Lake. Half of the lake lies in China's Occupied Territory of Aksai Chin. The color of the water changes according to the position of the sun in the sky. If you are a rockstar photographer then it is the best place for you. Pangong Tso Lake is located at 14200 feet and gives a sheer treat to your eyes. It is beautiful and popular for its picturesque landscapes with pure white arid mountains the background. Tso Moriri Lake in Ladakh is one of the beautiful, calm and sacred places which hidden away for the centuries. It is located at 14,836 feet which is highest in India and spectacular place for the Himalayan birds and animals. It’s just a heaven for travelers!

Tough Trekking in Ladakh

Leh Ladakh is called as the land of the monks and monasteries. It is famous for its beautiful locations at the top of the hills. It is the vantage point but the view is astonishing! However, the winds in the night can chill your bones. Trek up to the high altitude Spituk Monastery which can be adventurous and once in a lifetime experience. It will certainly excite you and thrill. Ladakh is popular among nature and adventure lovers with the number of Trekking Routes in Ladakh and trails for romance with nature. Trek on the picturesque trail will let you travel to the glacier, climbing on the mountains and let you through the long and steep ridge. Once you start Trekking in Ladakh you will explore the beautiful altitude passes, valleys, fauna and flora which make your experience larger than life. Experiencing the Bactrian Camel Safari in Ladakh can be a marvelous experience! This is the ancient Silk Route in ancient times where Caravan of traders used to carry trade between India, China, and Tibet. The Camel Ride in Ladakh is one of the exotic things to do in Ladakh.

Nature Tour in Ladakh

You can witness the Anti-gravity in Ladakh at Magnetic hills. The science behind this is still unknown. Even if you leave your car with the unlocked breaks then it will start moving the uphill slowly. It’s magic that you may never see before! It’s a dream place for Astrophotographers because it gives clear night skies to the accomplishment of the scenic sky full of stars. You can easily spot the Milky Way Galaxy in the world when cities are usually polluted with the dust and you can’t even see the stars in the night. Easy Cab India lets you experience the beauty of the Ladakh. Nubra Valley and Shyok Valley give special eye-widening views to every traveler.

Thrilling Mountain Biking in Ladakh

Khardung La Pass is one of the highest passes which lead beautiful Shyok Valley and Nubra Valley. Your bike ride filled with the excitement and thrill which go through the spectacular Himalayan Range. It is located at the 18379 feet and counted among the highest motorable biking roads in the world. It offers astonishing views and takes your experience at the highest level! Higher than the Khardung La Pass, the Uming La Pass is located at the 19300 feet which is dream pass for the motorbike adventures in Ladakh. It is the highest among all the passes with a lot of thrill and adventure. It stretches over 86 km and famous for being devoid of civilizations and lies completely barren.

Adventure Activities to Do in Ladakh

The River Rafting in Ladakh can be one of the finest adventures that swing your mood. It is one of the challenging water sports in India. The most popular river rafting in Ladakh is on River Rafting in Zanskar River where you see several ancient monasteries and villages. Even it allows the camp at villages like Nimmo. The Wildlife in the Ladakh will let you see the completely different side of it. Hemis National Park is the home of the fascinating wildlife and wild vegetation. The attraction of the park is snow leopard, shape sheep and vegetation of Ladakh. It is also famous for brown bears and Tibetan Wolf. You will spot donkeys in Leh Ladakh Donkey Sanctuary which is the only place where you will see the animals that not working anymore and it will give you hope and inspiration.

Cultural Tour in Ladakh (Buddhism, Food & Ethnic Influence)

The Leh Palace may be lost but the wood and stone ruins are interesting enough for a visit. It was built in the century and control under the Archeological Survey of India. It offers a panoramic view of the Stock Kangari and Zanskar Range. You will get the best cultural experience at the Hemis Festival. It is a two-day long festival that is celebrated on the birth anniversary of the Guru Padmasambhava. Furthermore, the mouthwatering dishes of the Indian, Tibetan, Korean and Chinese origin can make your all hunger satiate! You must try the Thukpa, Tsampa, Noodle Soup, and Skyu. The popular drinks are Tea, Coffee, and Chang that local people drink during the festive occasions.
