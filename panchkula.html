The name Panchkula is originated from the five ancient water canals that distribute water from the Ghaggar river to the other parts of the Haryana and thus fulfill the water needs of the people and for the irrigation of the field. From its beautiful greenery to pleasant atmosphere and famous dining and street food quality, people are being compelled to visit this beautiful place again and again. It covers some range of Shivalik Hills and has very famous ancient temples which add more beauty to the Panchkula. It is the border city of Punjab,Chandigarh and Himachal Pradesh, and this is the reason tourists visiting the nearby destination surely visit Panchkula.
