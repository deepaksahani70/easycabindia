  • Dal lake:-a small lake surrounded by cedar trees with a beautiful and calm scenic place.
  • St.John in the wilderness church:-a peaceful neo-gothic historic church lying between Dharamshal and McLeod Ganj.
  • HPCA cricket stadium:-one of the highest peak cricket stadiums in the world offering views of the kangra valleys.
  • Triund trek:-a popular trekking destination with panoramic mountain views.
  • Kangra fort :- a historic fort showcasing ancient architecture.
