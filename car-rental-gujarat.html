Taxi Service to Explore Gujarat's Famous Places

Easy Cab India offers various taxi service in Gujarat to meet individual customer needs, with daily and weekly car rentals available at its major tourist spots, such as:

Statue of Unity

Near Kevadia lies an impressive monument dedicated to Sardar Patel: 182 meters in height and located on an island in Narmada river, its viewing gallery at 153 meters will leave you speechless! Add laser light show, jungle safari nearby and river rafting for an exciting full-day adventure perfect for families, school groups or anyone curious to experience India's engineering excellence!

Somnath Temple

Lord Shiva's twelve Jyotirlingas, stand on the Arabian Sea coast. Though rebuilt several times since, its spirit still lives on today; for an ideal darshan experience be sure to visit around sunset; our drivers know when this happens so will arrive early enough!

Dwarkadhish Temple

Its main temple dates back 2,500 years; during evening aarti, thousands of lamps are illuminated as bells ring out in celebration - providing an energy beyond religious affiliation that pervades all aspects of Dwarka life.

Also worth seeing nearby are Bet Dwarka (where Krishna resided with his family), Nageshwar Jyotirlinga nearby, and Bet Dwarka itself - creating an ideal spiritual circuit between Somnath and Dwarka would require at least 3-4 days!

Rann of Kutch - White Desert

Kutch's famed white salt desert extends for miles into the distance, reflecting moonlight like an enormous mirror on full moon nights like an enormous mirror. From November to February tent cities offer cultural performances, local crafts, and Gujarati food; Kalo Dungar (Black Hill) offers incredible views across the Pakistan Border - perfect for photographers, couples or adventure tourism!

Gir National Park

Morning and evening safaris bring visitors into Gir's forest where these magnificent beasts roam free; Devalia Park provides a fenced zone within Gir where sightings almost guarantee themselves; ideal for wildlife enthusiasts, kids or those who have only seen caged lions before! Book with us to witness them for yourself!

Saputara Hill Station

Saputara Hill Station of Gujarat can be found in Dang District near Maharashtra border and features greenery, fresh air and clouds beneath your feet. Boating on Lake Saputara or taking the ropeway provides breathtaking valley views; sunset point offers romantic or family viewing experiences; summer temperatures typically hover between 25-30degC for optimal viewing experiences (compared with Ahmedabad temperatures which reach over 42degC!); Saputara makes an excellent weekend destination, college group trip destination or family outing destination with young children.

Mandvi Beach

Mandvi Beach in Bhuj is an underappreciated white sand beach that many Gujaratis don't realize exists despite offering ideal swimming conditions without dangerous waves common to Goa beaches. Not to mention an incredible 400-year-old shipbuilding yard where wooden ships are still handcrafted! Vijay Vilas Palace near Mandvi was featured prominently in Bollywood film Hum Dil De Chuke Sanam for providing clean beaches without overcrowding and providing pure relaxation!

Modhera Sun Temple

The grand stepwell (kund) still provides water today while its architecture allows sunlight to reach its main idol each morning and evening - an excellent spot for history enthusiasts, architecture students and photographers.

Polo Forest

Polo Forest in Vijapur, Gujarat dates back to the Solanki period of 10th-11th centuries and features stepwells, Jain temples and gateways from this period that have since been overgrown with forest over time. There's no entrance fee here: just you, monkeys and over a millennia of history for you to experience! Ideal for solo travelers seeking quiet solitude; groups seeking an alternate tourist experience; as well as anyone interested in discovering abandoned locations!

Champaner Pavagadh

Champaner Pavagadh near Vadodara is an incredible UNESCO World Heritage site with an iconic Kali temple that can be reached via cable car or 800 steps, while Champaner is home to Jama Masjid mosque - this medieval city has been carefully preserved here! Ideal for adventurers, history enthusiasts and families willing to walk a bit - come explore Champaner Pavagadh today!

Sabarmati Ashram

Sabarmati Ashram in Ahmedabad stands on the banks of Sabarmati River and Mahatma Gandhi made it a famous place when he resided here from 1917-1930 when he launched his Dandi March. A museum features letters, photos, and personal items belonging to Gandhiji. Relax on peaceful lawns featuring spinning wheels as you step back through time! Every Gujarati must visit at least once and travelers should not bypass visiting this important landmark!

Science City

Science City in Ahmedabad provides an engaging experience for children of all ages - especially the little ones! Attractions such as its Hall of Science, Energy Park, Space Theater and Musical Fountain Show make Science City especially captivating and appealing to visitors of all ages; children particularly enjoy visiting Dinosaur Park and Robotics Gallery which will keep them busy for hours upon hours - ideal for half day excursions with schoolgoing children! We provide custom sightseeing packages specifically tailored for visitors visiting Gujarat for three, five, or seven consecutive days. We make discovering beautiful sites easy!
