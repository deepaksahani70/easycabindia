Himachal Pradesh- It is the land of ( Beautifull Nature, Mountains, and Hills Station, etc )

Himachal offers natural beauty, walnuts, birches, and pines. It is the land where you can see the panoramic views of the mountains, hills, and valleys covered in the exotic flora. The land gives unbelievable imaginary experiences. It is the best place for trekking, mountain climbing, rafting, hiking, and other sports. Himachal Pradesh surrounded by summer retreats such as Shimla, Kullu, Dalhousie, and Manali. There are lots of places to visit and hold thrilling experience backgrounds of love, scandals, and war. You can visit from March to June. Luxury car travel takes you to one of the most adventurous trips of your life.

Hill stations in Himachal Pradesh

Manali is a hill station with attractions such as Rohtang Pass. Shimla is the capital city of Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for the Mall Road, Toy Train, The Road, and The Ridges. Mcleodganj and Dalhousie have some amazing scenic views. Dharmshala hill Station is the holy residence of the Dalai Lama. Kasauli is a hilly town which is best for a holiday location. Kinnaur is known as the “Land of Gods” and famous for lush green and mountainscape having Saltuj and Spiti River to it. Kufri has snow all around the winter which is the best holiday destination for the couples. Kullu offers panoramic views and majestic hills covered with pine trees.

Adventure activities to do in Himachal Pradesh

When you are heading to Himachal Pradesh then you must stop at Rohtang and Solang Valley. Skiing is the most popular sport here. Zorbing is also quite popular which lets you take advantage of the slopes of the valley. Zorbing is rolling down to slope in the huge transparent ball. Fly like a bird while Paragliding at Bir Billing. Rafting at Kullu Valley in Beas River is an adventurous experience ever! Camping at Mashobra and rock climbing at Vashist offers out in the wild experience. Mountain in Biking in Spiti will not be miss by the adventure lover.

Trekking in Himachal Pradesh

Kheer Ganga trek gives the most delightful experience ever! Trekking in Great Himalayan National Park is famous among nature lovers. Prashar Lake Trek and Indrahar Pass Track offer the most picturesque trail. For challenging adventure Pin Parvati Pass is the best destination for the trekkers. Pir Punjab Trek is just heaven for trekkers to offer some spectacular views. Beginners can start trekking at the Sar Pass Trek which passes through thick forest, lush green and snow Cloud Mountains. Chandratal is one of the high altitude lakes that give the perfect destination for trekking.

Wildlife in Himachal Pradesh

One of the best things in the presence of animals with the Himalayas makes the Wildlife of Himachal Pradesh Special. You can witness the Snow Leopard, Black Bear, Goral, and Sambhar. Even you can find the Musk Deer, Monal and Brown bear. Chail Wild Life Sanctuary is a true haven for wildlife enthusiastic! There are several famous sanctuaries where you can witness at Renuka Wildlife Sanctuary, Kunawar Sanctuary, Simbala Sanctuary, and Great Himalayan National Park is the best for it. Explore nature’s bounty and make your trip wild!

Lakes in Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Palaces have several places for the Honeymoon. Visit the fabulous lakes throughout the state which is full of beauty. Brighu Lake is popular among national and international tourists. Karari Lake is clysters clear lake and popular for a picnic spot. Maharana Pratap Lake is a man-made lake in India and 25 international wetland sites in India. Chandratal Lake offers fascinating and connected legends of the Mahabharata. Dal Lake and Dashir Lake counted among the major tourist attraction. Make your trip memorable!

Heritage places to visit in Himachal Pradesh

Hidimba temple in Manali is a peaceful place surrounded by the towering trees. Key Monestry is one of the oldest monasteries in Spili. Chokling monastery is the masterpiece in Bir. Kalka Shimla Railway is what the British left behind. Kangra fort and Jaitak fort is also popular among the tourists. Baijnath Temple and Chamunda Devi Temple are most visited by the devotees and tourists. Mandi is known as the ‘Choti Kashi.’ It has as many as 81 temples all across the area.

Toy train

A toy train journey from Kalka to Shimla is worth enjoying every second! The smell of the clear dew, fresh possible air to breathe in and mesmerizing view of the countryside make your experience sky high!
