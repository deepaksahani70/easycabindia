Best Places to See in Himachal

Himachal is seriously lovely. Not overrated. Put it on your list. You get clean air, big mountains, and a tiredness that feels good.

  • Shimla and Manali are famous for actual reasons. Snow in winter. Long walks. Rivers cutting through valleys that make you stay quiet for a minute. Shimla has the Ridge. Jakhoo temple. Kufri for horse rides. Manali has Solang Valley for paragliding. Old Manali for cheap cafes. Rohtang Pass if you want snow — but check if it's open first.
  • Then Dharamsala is So peaceful that people forget to leave. Mcleodganj shows you small lanes, Tibetan flags everywhere, and monks walking slowly. Kullu gives you shawls and apples. Spiti gives you nothing but mountains, sky, and that strange peace.
  • Dalhousie has old houses from British times. Khajjiar looks like a small Switzerland — just grass, trees, and a small lake. Kasol is full of backpackers, Israeli food, and walks along Parvati river.
  • Chamba has temples made thousands of years ago. Palampur has tea gardens where morning feels like a dream. Bir has paragliding and Buddhist monasteries.

With a good taxi service in Himachal Pradesh, you can visit all these. No hurry. No fixed plan. Just you and the hills.
