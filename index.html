What to choose: a car with Chauffeur or Self-Drive?

The car driven by a chauffeur is considered the least stressful option. However, driving yourself does bring some freedoms as well. Below we have briefly compared some of the factors you may consider while deciding what travel option suits you best in India.

Choose a Chauffeur If:

  • You want to have the option to be completely stress-free: First, you have to deal with the stress of traffic, then you have to deal with that stress again to find a parking space. Not even considering the multiple stresses of being lost, and the stress of driving. Let someone else deal with that.
  • You are travelling for business: You have the option to make calls or even work while being driven on your journey.
  • If you have safety concerned: Don't worry your chauffeur is familiar with the road condition. He will be native with the local language and the regional driving etiquette.

Choose Self Drive If:

  • You have a high need for privacy: Your space is completely guaranteed without having any strangers in the car with you.
  • You want to be spontaneous: You are free to stop whenever you want and detour completely. Also, there is no set schedule.
  • You want to save money: There is no need to pay a driver and there are absolutely no hidden costs (unlike with a chauffeur).
